About me

I'm Isabella — a business student at FAU and a current intern at Cox Automotive. I went into this internship with little to no corporate experience and honestly that was the point. I wanted to throw myself into something real and see what I was capable of. This is how I choose to live my everyday life. Push yourself to do difficult things. Outside of work you'll find me deep in my wellness routine. Finding my love for Pilates changed everything for me. Health, wellness, mind and body are very important to me. I choose to romanticize my day by doing what works for me and making a routine. If I had one piece of advice to give to someone trying to reach a goal of any kind, it would be: “Discipline is the number one tool to becoming your dream self. Discipline leads to motivation, motivation leads to consistency, and consistency leads to results.”

      🧠 My non-negotiables

      🏃

      I don't skip movement

      Even on the lowest days I find a way to move — a walk, 10 minutes outside, Pilates, lifting. It doesn't have to be big. It just has to happen. Movement is not optional for me, it's how I stay sane.

      BODY
      ☀️

      I protect my morning

      My morning routine is the foundation of my entire day. Prebiotic drink, bone broth, vibration plate, high protein breakfast — in that order. If my morning is solid, everything else is easier. I don't negotiate this.

      RITUAL
      🥩

      I hit my protein goal every day

      This is non-negotiable for me whether I'm lifting, doing Pilates, or just going about my day. Protein keeps me full, keeps my energy stable, and supports everything I'm working toward physically. No exceptions.

      NUTRITION
      🚶

      I walk outside for at least 30 minutes

      This is on my good day checklist and I protect it. Walking outside — not on a treadmill, actually outside — clears my head more than almost anything else. It's when I decompress, think, and reset.

      RESET
      💧

      I drink my water

      Hydration is foundational and I treat it that way. Prebiotic drink on an empty stomach, bone broth, water throughout the day. It sounds simple but consistency with hydration changes your skin, energy, and digestion more than people realise.

      HEALTH
      🛏️

      I make my bed every single morning

      This is the first thing I do. Every day. It takes two minutes and it starts my day with a win before anything else has happened. It's not about the bed — it's about how it makes me feel walking out of that room.

      MINDSET
      📋

      I plan my day before I live it

      I time block. I make step-by-step plans. Having my day mapped out takes the anxiety away completely. When I know what I'm doing and when, I don't spend energy worrying about it — I just execute it.

      DISCIPLINE
      ⏸️

      I take at least one hour of real rest

      Not scrolling-rest. Not half-watching something while answering messages. Actual rest. This is on my non-negotiable checklist for a good day. Rest is part of the plan — not the reward for finishing everything else.

      RECOVERY
      🧠

      I tell myself to slow down

      I catch myself rushing constantly — eating, moving, getting things done. So I literally talk to myself. Out loud. "Slow down." "You're okay." "Breathe." This is not weird — this is how I stay in my body instead of just in my head.

      AWARENESS
      👨‍👩‍👧

      I spend time with the people I love

      This is on my good day checklist. No matter how busy or productive I am, connection with people I love is not something I sacrifice. A good day includes people. That's just who I am.
